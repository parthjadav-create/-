No, the unemployment rate did not fall to a 45-year low under this government

A widely shared claim attributes a historic employment record to the current administration. Official CMIE and NSSO data tell a different story — one that has been quietly revised, delayed, and contested.

"Our government has achieved the lowest unemployment rate in 45 years — proof that our economic vision is working for every Indian." — Political rally speech, March 2025 · Verified by multiple media clips

The claim relies on selectively quoting the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) while ignoring the suppression of the 2017–18 NSS employment report, which showed unemployment at a 45-year high. Both the CMIE and independent economists have challenged the methodology.

By Editorial Team | Sources: PLFS 2023–24, CMIE, NSC Report 2019 | Updated 22 May 2025
